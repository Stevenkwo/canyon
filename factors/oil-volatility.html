Oil volatility (OVX), daily

The Cboe crude oil volatility index, read against a single line at 50. A close that drops below 50 switches on the R1 USO leg for 21 trading days.

What it measures#

OVX is the Cboe Crude Oil ETF Volatility Index: the 30-day implied volatility of options on USO, the crude oil fund, quoted as an annualized percentage. A reading of 50 means the options market prices a one-month move in USO of roughly ±14%. The daily value is fixed at 15:15 ET, before the equity close, which is what lets the rule act on the same day.

Since 2007 the median close is 36, and 15% of days have closed at 50 or above. This year ran from 28.4 on Jan 2 to 120.9 on Mar 11.

The leg triggers when the close moves from 50 or above to below 50, buys at that close, holds 21 trading days and sells at the close. It cannot trigger again while holding, and needs a close at 50 or above before the next trigger.

The Oct 29 close is 57.2. The index dropped below 50 on Oct 5 (48.65, from 51.00 the day before), stayed under 50 for nine sessions, crossed back above on Oct 16 (50.74), peaked at 60.02 on Oct 23 and has eased a little since. That is the 92nd percentile of all closes since 2007 and the 60th within the past year, a year that includes the March spike.

How the rule reads it#

Level Rule What happens
Close moves from 50 or above to below 50 R1 USO leg Buy USO, 20% of NAV, at that day's close
21 trading days later R1 USO leg Sell at the close, whatever OVX did in between
While holding No new trigger
After the sale A close at 50 or above re-arms the leg

The 50 line is a reference line, not a forecast. The leg buys a drop in fear after a spell of fear, and holds for a fixed window. The evidence behind it is thin:

  • Declustered triggers from 2007 to 2026 (37 completed): 65% positive over the 21 days, +2.6% on average, t = 1.1. Over ten years (24 triggers): 62%, +1.9%, t = 0.6. Not significant, so the leg is registered as a probe under monitoring rather than a core leg.
  • USO itself loses about 6% a year since inception, most of it from rolling futures in contango, so the leg holds only inside the window and never sits in USO between triggers.
  • The false signal on record is Mar 2, 2020: a cross below 50 just before the crash, followed by −57.6% in USO over the next 21 days.
  • A variant that bought XOM on a cross below 45 was tested and refuted.
