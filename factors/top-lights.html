The rule#

Each light compares a market measure with its own past year, so it comes on only when that measure is at an extreme for the last 252 sessions.

Light On when What it is looking for
① VIX × NDX divergence Nasdaq-100 within 2% of its 60-day high and VIX above its one-year 60th percentile Index near the top while insurance gets more expensive
② Mag7 dispersion Gap between the best and worst 21-day Mag7 return above its one-year 80th percentile Leadership splitting apart
③ Small caps beating the Nasdaq IWM minus Nasdaq-100, 21 days, above its one-year 85th percentile Money rotating away from the leaders
④ NVDA call IV At-the-money implied volatility of NVDA calls 20+ days out above 60% Speculative demand for upside in the bellwether
⑤ 0DTE share Above 60% of options volume Not counted here: no live source
⑥ Semiconductor breadth Chip index within 2% of its 60-day high while the share of 8 chip names above their 50-day average is below its one-year 40th percentile A rally carried by fewer and fewer names
  • Three or more lit at the same time is the rule's line. Three without ① or ⑥ counts as 2.5.
  • Two or more lit on at least 3 of the last 5 days is the early warning.
  • ① and ⑥ only fire near a high. When the index is well below its high they stay off by design, which is not the same as safe.

How it is updated#

  • Every 15 minutes from 9:35 to 16:20 New York time on trading days, a script on the author's machine recomputes the lights from Yahoo prices, which can be up to 15 minutes old, and republishes this page. GitHub Pages adds a minute or two.
  • Percentile lines are recomputed on every run from the trailing 252 sessions, including today's price.
  • Offline: if the machine is off, the page keeps the last reading and its time stamp.
  • Track record, recomputed over 2006–2026: three or more of ① ② ③ ⑥ were on together on 23 days in 7 episodes, and only one of eight 15%+ Nasdaq-100 declines (February 2020) was preceded by three lights. As a top signal the rule is weak. The same lights do better at a different job, flagging how rough the next three months get once volatility is up; that is F5, the turbulence gauge.

Disclosure

How this reading is usedNo position is held on this reading. Three or more lights at once is the account's reduce-risk line, and ① and ⑥ are its hard signals. Between updates the page shows the last reading and its time stamp.

Readings are computed from public prices with the stated definition. They describe the past and say nothing certain about the future. Notes on this site express the author's opinions.
