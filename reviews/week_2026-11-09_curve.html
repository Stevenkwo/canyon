Positions carried from the prior close lost $815.10. Same-day transactions, marked to the close and net of fees, added $2,979.46. AAPL (+$2,059.03) and FTNT (+$1,123.21) led the gains. NVDA (−$2,332.93) and SQQQ (−$572.11) were the largest losses. The NVDA position carried into November 10 alone lost $2,934 before that day's fills reduced the loss.
