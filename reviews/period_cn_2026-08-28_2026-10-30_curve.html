Review notes

Nine weeks in one paragraph

From the Aug 28 close to Oct 30 the account made +3.9% in net-asset terms (+¥88,571 of P&L) while the CSI 300 lost 13.5%, the SSE Composite 8.9% and QQQ 3.5%. The Aug 28 book left untouched would have made +3.3%; chaining each week's opening book instead gives −1.7%. So the nine weeks, taken together, were roughly a draw against doing nothing with the starting book, and clearly better than the index. The money came from two legs that were not traded much: the S&P Oil & Gas ETF (+¥58,907, of which ¥53,106 was earned overnight) and Shaanxi Coal (+¥58,053, ¥39,924 overnight). The money went out through the Dow Jones ETF (−¥25,715) and the Nikkei 225 ETF (−¥19,354, a name outside the pool), and through fees of ¥25,602 on 731 fills, 44 times the book's value in turnover.

How to read the percentages

Each recorded week is re-based on the engine's net assets for that week, and those were corrected twice by position statements (Sep 18 and Oct 9). A correction is not a return, so it is absorbed between weeks rather than drawn as a move. The side effect: a month's money and its percentage can disagree in sign. September made +¥6,570 but reads −0.7%, because the losing weeks were measured against a smaller (estimated) base than the winning weeks. The week of Oct 19 was not recorded; the shaded band shows what the carried book alone would have done (−4.9%). From Oct 16 the book is an estimate.

What the period says

  • The hand has not added. Over nine weeks the same-day hand (fees included) is slightly negative; what was carried overnight is where the result lives. That is the account's own ledger confirming the rule book's default of no action.
  • Turnover is the cost line. 44× the book in nine weeks, ¥25,602 in fees, 700+ fills; the five checks were never met on fill count or first-half-hour fills in any week.
  • Structure arrived last. Two legs in band only in the final week, and only by selling the frozen Nasdaq-100 ETF.
  • Records are the gap. Two statements in nine weeks; one week with no recording. The next statement re-bases everything after Oct 16.
