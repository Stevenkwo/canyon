The week in one paragraph

AAPL (+$3,446, mostly same-day opens and MOC exits), FTNT (+$2,215, capturing Wednesday's −2.8% gap trigger for +$1,557), QQQ (+$1,448, carried +$3,406 offset by −$1,958 intraday churn), and LLY (+$497) drove gains. Short BABA was the largest loser (−$1,701: carried −$2,343 across Monday and Friday gaps vs +$642 intraday), followed by GOOG (−$399, carried −$1,318 vs hand +$919) and NVDA (−$382, carried −$1,934 vs hand +$1,552).
