Review notes

Ten weeks in one paragraph

From the Aug 28 close to Nov 6 the account is down 7.4% in net-asset terms (−¥113,071 of P&L) while the CSI 300 lost 12.0%, the SSE Composite 7.7% and QQQ 8.3%. The Aug 28 book left untouched would have lost 8.4%; chaining each week's opening book instead gives −12.2%. One week undid nine: the ten weeks stood at +3.9% on Oct 30 and the week of Nov 2 took −10.9% (−¥201,642), almost all of it from the carried book itself (no-trade −10.7%). The two legs that made the money through October, the S&P Oil & Gas ETF and Shaanxi Coal, were the two that lost the most in November: the oil ETF fell 17.7% in the week while the position in it was raised by 170,900 units, and coal fell 7.9%.

How to read the percentages

Each recorded week is re-based on the engine's net assets for that week, and those were corrected by position statements on Sep 18 and Oct 9. A correction is not a return, so it is absorbed between weeks rather than drawn as a move; a month's money and its percentage can therefore disagree in sign (September made +¥6,570 but reads −0.7%). The week of Oct 19 was not recorded; the shaded band shows what the carried book alone would have done. No statement has been taken since Oct 9, so from Oct 16 the book, debt and collateral are estimates inferred from fills, and the last-month block on this page is anchored at the Oct 30 close.

What the period says

  • The result lives in the carried book, in both directions. Over ten weeks the same-day hand (fees included) is small; October's gain and November's loss were both what the overnight book did. The hand did not add in the good weeks and did not protect in the bad one.
  • Turnover is the cost line. 976 fills in ten weeks, ¥34,022 in fees, turnover of about 60× the book; the five checks were never met on fill count or first-half-hour fills in any week, and the week of Nov 2 set the record at 245 fills.
  • Leverage rose into the loss. Estimated margin debt went from about ¥718k to about ¥1.17m during the week of Nov 2 (¥453k of net buying), above the ¥1m limit, while the book fell. The limit is a survival rule, not a performance rule.
  • Records are the gap. Two statements in ten weeks, one unrecorded week, and three weeks of book inferred from fills. The next statement re-bases everything after Oct 16.
