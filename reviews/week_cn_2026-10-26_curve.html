The week in one paragraph

  • Activity: 136 fills, ¥17.7m of turnover (7.1× the book), 33 fills in the first half hour, 45 fills on Friday alone. The worst round trips were Nasdaq-100 ETF batches bought Wednesday and Thursday afternoon and sold Friday morning after QQQ's −3.5% night.
