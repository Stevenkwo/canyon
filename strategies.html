Strategies

The rule sets behind the account. Each page gives the rules as they are registered, the sizing, a ten-year backtest, the conditions that would switch the rules off, and whether they are in force or on paper.

R1
NVDA by night, AAPL by day

Two auction orders on each side of the session. Buy NVDA at the close and hold it overnight, swap it for AAPL at the open, sell AAPL at the close. Two gated energy legs and an FTNT gap-down overlay sit beside it.

In forceIn force since Oct 26, 2026 · rule change #17
Ten-year backtest: +85.9% a year · Sharpe 2.05 · max drawdown −39.7%
R3
Half the NVDA through the day (draft)

A draft variant of R1. Keep half the NVDA through the session, double AAPL on intraday margin, and add a half slot for FTNT, TSLA or a BABA short. It is tracked on paper until Nov 26, 2026.

Off-peak: Shaanxi Coal and the Nasdaq-100

A coal stock and the Nasdaq-100 tend to peak at different times. One rule in force nudges the coal position inside a ±10% band. A research rule moves 20 points of a 50/50 pair into the Nasdaq-100 when coal runs hot, and moves them back when it cools.
